UNIVERSE

hold’em, played with a deck on three axes

An ordinary deck varies on two axes: suit × rank. This one adds a third — three colors, four shapes, five numbers, sixty cards, every combination exactly once.

color purple · green · yellow
shapeeye · helix · pyramid · star
number1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5

Two cards to you, three to the table, turned one at a time. Your hand is your two and all three — five cards, never a choice of which five, so every hand you hold is one of the nineteen on the chart at exactly the rarity printed beside it.

There is no high-card hand. Only five numbers exist, so five cards without a repeat are already the whole run.
