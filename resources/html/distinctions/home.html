DISTINCTIONS

six distinctions, two ways each, sixty-four cards

Everyone is dealt eight cards and one card is turned up to start the pile. On your turn take the pile's top card or draw from the deck, then throw one card onto the pile.

You win the moment your hand is two teams of four: one team agrees on three distinctions, the other on the other three, so between them your hand covers all six. Play against bots, other people, or alone against the turn count.
