How to read this

Each candidate plays the same frozen opponents with the same seeds and 64 search simulations. Bars use all scheduled games as their scale. Compare completed rows against the same opponent; partial results can be biased toward shorter games. Each opponent gets 32 games for 2p or 48 for 3p, balanced across seats. In 3p the other two seats use copies of that opponent. These are benchmark results, not an overall rating.

    Reading the charts & controls

    Outcome bars show self-play endings per iteration, not strength against an opponent. Hover or focus a chart for counts and ranges. Evaluations use frozen opponents; their model iteration is shown. Cutoffs are separate from victories. Loss alone does not measure playing strength.

    Gold outlines mark changed spaces. Arrow keys step; Space plays or pauses. Scrubbing pauses the live feed. Power bars show progress toward the power required to win (5 in the training rules). Piece bars compare current piece counts. Three living organisms can also win independently of power. The history chart covers the loaded recording or live buffer.
