# OVERVIEW

We have landed on a planet in a new solar system \- we have barely survived, and even now have dire effort before we are secure. To begin, we need to bootstrap our own energetic solar network through planetary mining to power our cities and ultimately establish trade routes between the planets. As we go our solar network will grow into the throbbing heart of a new civilization (\!(\!\!\!))

# COMPONENTS

* One game board with 6 rings: Sun/silver/green/blue/purple/void. Each space is adjacent to the next space CW and CCW of it in the same ring, and also any space in a ring above or below whose side overlaps with that space. In each ring, “forward” means CCW and backwards means CW.   
  * Sun with 5 spaces (wedges), each with an inner “active” part (closer to the center, 1 for each planet color, starting with silver at the beam and going in color order CW: silver/green/blue/purple/void) and an outer “exhausted” part (further from the center, uniformly red)  
  * 5 orbital rings, each with a planet of its color and a characteristic orbital number (going from in to out, closest to the sun to furthest in color order):  
    * Silver \- 5 spaces  
    * Green \- 8 spaces  
    * Blue \- 13 spaces  
    * Purple \- 21 spaces  
    * Void \- 34 spaces  
  * BEAM: The first space of each ring is part of the BEAM, starting from the sun and going through the mid-point of each space.   
* Orbital dice  
  * 1d4: 1-4  
  * 1d10: 0-9  
* Deck of 78 cards (6 suits of 13 cards each)  
  * Solar flare  
  * silver/green/blue/purple/void  
* Energy, 89  
* Market, for each of the 5 planet colors:  
  * Market token  
  * 8 resources  
* Cities, 4 in each of the 5 planet colors  
* Flame of Justice  
* 5 sets of player pieces, each player has:  
  * 1 habitat  
  * 1 mothership  
  * 13 links  
  * 13 sundivers  
  * 8 components  
  * 5 city platforms

# SETUP

* Lay the board open  
* Shuffle the deck and place it next to the board.   
* Place each market token in a column \- each market will extend out horizontally from each market token. One side is the resources side and the other side is the city side.   
* At setup there are this number of resources of each color in their respective rows, placed on the resource side:  
  * Silver \- 1  
  * Green \- 2  
  * Blue \- 3  
  * Purple \- 4  
  * Void \- 5  
* Each player takes all player pieces of one color  
  * Set up your habitat  
  * Place 8 sundivers in habitat  
  * Take 5 energy into habitat  
  * The other 5 sundivers are placed with the unrealized components and city platforms into a reserve  
* Roll the orbital dice. d4 is 10s and d10 is 1s:  
  * One by one, start each planet on the beam of its respective orbital ring  
  * Find the remainder after dividing die roll by its orbital number \- advance the planet that many spaces along its orbital ring  
  * Use the same roll for all planets  
* Give the flame of justice to a random player, or the most hostile player:   
  * They place one component of their color on the silver wedge of the sun in the active half.   
  * Each subsequent player places one component of their color on the next wedge (active also)

# GAMEPLAY

The player’s mothership is always in one space on the board pointing counterclockwise. The space directly counterclockwise is considered the “front” of the mothership. 

Each turn, the player **resolves** their mothership:

* If a player’s mothership is not on the board, they place it on any of the 5 orbital spaces along the beam.   
* Otherwise, they choose whether to go out one ring, in one ring, or stay in the ring they are in (\!) If you change orbital rings you must go to the first space in front of the mothership up or down, and move the flame of justice into the space that is now in front of your mothership. 

Then they choose one of the following options:

* Move  
* Activate

If activation is chosen, you must activate at least one thing (planet/sun/city). A number of cards may be drawn based on activation, draw and resolve these cards one at a time \- if moving instead, draw 1 card. Any flares drawn, the player’s mothership is pulled forward/down one orbital ring towards the sun. If this would pull the player into the sun, they move forward in the silver ring instead and lose half of their energy rounded up. 

If the 13th flare is drawn, the game ends immediately \- proceed to final scoring. 

Whatever the last card drawn was determines which planet orbits: each planet color has a corresponding card which makes it orbit, and flares advance all planets. 

To orbit a planet, move it clockwise a number of spaces:

* Silver \- 1  
* Green \- 1  
* Blue \- 1  
* Purple \- 2  
* Void \- 3

Finally, advance your mothership to the space with the flame of justice, and place the flame of justice in front of the mothership of the player next in turn order (CW). 

## MOVE

Everyone have 5 movement points on their turn. For one move you can:

* Launch from the habitat onto one of 4 spaces:  
  * space the mothership is in  
  * the space in front of the mothership  
  * The space to either side (up or down one ring) to the *front* of the mothership  
* Fly a sundiver already on the board to an adjacent space (including into one of the 5 spaces in the sun)  
* PATHS: A path is a continuous set of links of the same player. If a sundiver starts on a space with links, travel along a path to any space on that same path (even through cities). If it is another player’s path, they gain 1 energy.

   
During movement, if your sundiver is in a space with a planet it is free to place your sundiver on or off of the planet \- you must choose before cards are drawn and any planets orbit. 

You can choose to decline any or all of your movement points. If you choose to use zero moves you are effectively passing, but you still draw 1 card. 

## ACTIVATE

When you activate, choose one of the following targets to activate:

* Sun  
* Planets  
* Cities

You may activate all spaces of that type where you have sundivers. You can activate each space once per turn, no matter how many sundivers you have on it. 

Each planet or sun space you activate you will draw a card for \- each city you activate you draw cards based on its level (how many total cities are in that ring). Draw them after all of your activations are complete. 

### PLANETS

You may activate a sundiver on a planet in one of two ways:

Without a resource: 

* pay to the market the energy required and receive one resource you place under that sundiver. The energy required depends on how many are in the market of that color:  
  * 1 \- 5 energy  
  * 2 \- 3 energy  
  * 3 \- 2 energy  
  * 4 \- 1 energy  
  * 5 \- 1 energy  
* There will never be more than 5 energy remaining, or less than zero (\!)

With a resource of a different color than the planet you are on, AND if there is no city of that color in this ring already (as indicated by disks on the city side of the market), AND there is no city in that space:

* Vaporize the sundiver (it may never return)  
* Place its resource on the market row corresponding to the ring of the planet on the city side.   
* Place one of your city platforms in the space.  
* Place a city of the color of the resource on the platform.   
* Replenish the resources of that row up to 5 (\!) if there are not enough remaining, replenish as much as you can. 

### SUN

There are five spaces in the sun, each divided into two sections, and active section of a particular planet color, and an exhausted section that is red. “Active” and “exhausted” sections are the same space for adjacency purposes, but sundivers without resources are placed in the exhausted section, and sundivers with resources are placed in the active section (for clarity). 

Components are active if they are in the active half of the space, and exhausted if they are in the exhausted half. 

To activate an outer red section:

* Return the activating sundiver to your habitat  
* Add 2 energy \+ 1 for each active component and \+ 2 for each exhausted component to your habitat.   
* Move all exhausted components from this space’s exhausted section to its active half (they are now “active”). 

If you have a sundiver with a resource of the same color as the space you are in, you can activate the inner sun space:

* Return the resource and activating sundiver to reserve.  
* Place a component of your color in the active section of the space. 

### CITIES

When activating a city, return the activating sundiver on that city to your habitat. You get a number of city actions based on the level and owner of the city. The level is the number of cities in that ring total (including the one you are activating) \- you can count the number of disks on the city side of the market for this ring. 

Based on the level you get the following number of actions:

* Level 1 \- 1 base / 1 bonus  
* Level 2 \- 2 base / 1 bonus  
* Level 3 \- 3 base / 2 bonus

The activator takes the base number of actions, then the owner has the choice to take the bonus. If the owner declines, the activator can take the bonus instead, or decline themselves. 

Activate each city one at a time. 

Each city action places one link, and requires:

* Spend one energy  
* Color Access: an exhausted component of the color of sun space or city you are linking out of. You can exhaust one component for your first link and then anyone can use the same color for their links this turn if they wish. If you want to grow out of a different wedge of the sun or a different color of city, you have to exhaust a component of that color. If you exhaust a component of another player, they receive one energy for every link you create using that color. 

A link is placed between two spaces. All links must trace back to the sun. A space is “saturated” with links for a player if they already have two links into/out of that space. To place a new link: 

* Choose a starting space that is either \-  
  * a sun space or   
    * a city or   
    * a link connected directly to a city   
  * of a color you have access to.   
  * That is not saturated with your links  
  * That has an open connection available (only one link of any player between any two spaces) to a space you haven’t saturated. 

# ENDING

After the 13th flare is drawn, the game ends immediately. 

CONNECTIONS: A connection is a section of a player’s path that connects a city back to the sun, or to a city that itself is connected back to the sun through any number of other cities. 

Every player scores their valid connections. A connection is valid for that player if each end is either: 

* A city on a platform of their color.   
* A city of a color they have a solar component of.   
* A solar space they have a component in. 

A player’s score is the total number of valid connections they created. For each end of the connection that is valid because it is a city of another player that matches the color of one of their components, they get an additional point for each component beyond 1 they have of that color. 

The player with the highest score is the winner (\!) Any tied players lose, and the next highest score is the winner (\!) If no one is untied, everyone loses (except in the case of SALVATION)

## SALVATION

If all players end with the same number of:

* Points  
* Components  
* Cities

Then the players have achieved a communal victory, broken the cycle of destruction and saved humanity and the future (\!\!\!)
